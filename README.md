# Talkie

Talkie is an iPhone app that helps people with ALS, people who are non-speaking, and people who have trouble speaking take part in conversations.

**[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6761003971)**

## What it does

- Listens to the people around you and transcribes what they say, on the device.
- Suggests replies you can pick with one tap, using Apple Intelligence on the device. You can also switch to Claude with your own Anthropic API key.
- Speaks your reply out loud, with the system voice or a clone of your own voice made with ElevenLabs.
- Recognizes who is talking, so suggestions fit the person in front of you, including in group conversations.
- Lets you write longer texts ahead of time and read them out later.

On-device reply suggestions need iOS 26 or later with Apple Intelligence turned on. Without it, you can still type your replies.

## Privacy

Talkie has no server, no analytics and no ads. Transcripts, your profile and speaker voiceprints stay on your iPhone. Data only leaves the device if you turn on ElevenLabs or Claude yourself. The full policy explains what is sent and when.
